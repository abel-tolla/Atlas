# User Personas

These four personas are working hypotheses built from the planning sessions, not research findings. Spike 4 (testing with 3 to 5 people) should confirm or change them.

## Maya, the overloaded student

**Who:** 20, junior, full course load plus a part-time job.

**Calendars:** Canvas for classes and deadlines, Google for work shifts, Outlook for a campus club, Apple for personal plans.

**Goals:** Never miss a deadline. Know whether an assignment fits before it is due. Study without double-booking her shifts.

**Frustrations:** Deadlines buried in Canvas. Four apps to check each morning. Study plans she makes by hand and abandons.

**How she uses Atlas:** Pastes an assignment, reviews the tasks and blocks the AI proposes, and taps a block done each evening. Her estimates improve over the term.

## Daniel, the meeting-heavy professional

**Who:** 29, software engineer at a mid-size company.

**Calendars:** Outlook for work meetings, Google for personal life.

**Goals:** Protect focus time. Prepare for demo days and release dates without last-minute crunch. Keep work from taking over his evenings.

**Frustrations:** Meetings fragment his day. Reminders from two apps that do not know about each other.

**How he uses Atlas:** Sets up zones (Work, Sleep, Leisure), attaches prep tasks to a demo day event, and lets the AI propose focus blocks between meetings.

## Priya, the routine builder

**Who:** 26, marketing coordinator who trains five days a week.

**Calendars:** Google for work, Apple for personal.

**Goals:** Follow a push, pull, legs, rest split without it breaking when life gets in the way. Fit meal prep and gym around a changing schedule.

**Frustrations:** Calendar apps only repeat on fixed weekdays, so one missed session throws off the whole week.

**How she uses Atlas:** Creates a four-day rolling cycle that shifts when she misses a day. Pauses it during a trip. Gets alerts a day before and an hour before.

## Jordan, the multi-calendar juggler

**Who:** 34, project manager and parent.

**Calendars:** Outlook for work, Google for a side business, a shared Apple calendar for the family.

**Goals:** One view of everything. Decide for himself which clash wins, such as a school event over a status meeting. Get five alerts ahead of a big presentation, not two.

**Frustrations:** Some calendar apps cap alerts at two, and he resolves double-bookings in three different places.

**How he uses Atlas:** Lives in the Today view and its inbox. Marks events that allow overlap. Hides noise from the shared calendar and can still see why it was hidden.
